## Image Optimization Guidelines

### Production Optimization

1. **Convert to WebP** after generation for better compression (50-80% smaller than JPEG)
2. **Generate 2x versions** (@2x) for retina displays
3. **Implement lazy loading** for images below the fold
4. **Use responsive images** with srcset for different screen sizes

### Compression Settings

- **JPEG Quality:** 85-90% for hero images, 75-85% for gallery
- **WebP Quality:** 80-85% (provides better compression than JPEG)
- **Maintain aspect ratios** to prevent layout shifts

### Responsive Sizes Needed

- **Mobile:** 640px width
- **Tablet:** 1024px width
- **Desktop:** 1920px width (full size)

## AI Image Generation Tips

### For Best Results:

1. **Mention:** "hyper-realistic", "professional photography", "8k quality"
2. **Specify lighting:** "natural lighting", "golden hour", "warm ambient lighting"
3. **Include context:** Dubai setting, luxury environment, premium materials
4. **Avoid:** Text, logos, watermarks, distorted faces or hands
5. **Style keywords:** "commercial photography", "editorial quality", "cinematic composition"

### Post-Processing:

- Adjust white balance for consistency across images
- Ensure color grading matches the luxury brand aesthetic
- Check for proper exposure and contrast
- Remove any AI artifacts or imperfections
